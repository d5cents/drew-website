**ABOUT**

Drew Nichols’ career as a commercial artist began at age 4, when he received a police escort home after selling handmade paper bag puppets door-to-door. He went on to study Music Composition at Arizona State University, where his first musical _Invincible_ premiered with an orchestra of 23 players. He gained broad experience in theatre, interning in the music library at The Phoenix Symphony_,_ interning in Company Management at Glimmerglass _Opera_, playing keyboard (_ALW’s The Wizard of Oz_ tour_)_ and rehearsal piano (_Hadestown_ Broadway/National_)_, music directing/playing at regional houses across the country _(_Tuacahn, Engeman, Lake Dillon) and schools around NYC, orchestrating several full musicals (_Of Leto, The Light Rail_ for others, and his own _Invincible, McCobb Mortality Services_), contributing orchestrations to benefits (Broadway Backwards, Westport County Playhouse), writing/producing for _Rathskeller: A Musical Elixir,_ and writing dance arrangements for _Beyond Perfection_ (both off-off-Broadway. He has done extensive work as an Associate Keyboard Programmer for Randy Cohen Keyboards: _Rocky Horror Show_ (Broadway), _Maybe Happy Ending_ (tour), _Iceboy!_ (Goodman), _The Lunchbox_ (Berkeley Rep), _Ever After_ (Phoenix Theatre) _Safety Not Guaranteed_ (Signature), _Working Girl_ (La Jolla), Jesus Christ Superstar (_Hollywood Bowl_), _Water For Elephants_ (tour), _Suffs_ (tour); and Associate/Assistant Keyboard Programmer for Stuart Andrews: _Death Becomes Her_ (Chicago, Broadway), _Sunset Boulevard_ (Broadway), _Cats_ (tour) and _Jesus Christ Superstar Live_ (NBC). 

His musical McCobb Mortality Services was adapted into a six episode Radio Musical Comedy (podcast), along with an Original Radio Cast Recording.  He’s now teamed up with Emily Boyd Dahab on a thorough rewrite, still in progress, but will be ready for workshopping again within a year.

_The Likely Heroes,_ his musical with Jessie Kleinman and Ryan Kleinman, has received four informal readings of the book, as well as demos of many of the songs. It’s ready for formal reading/workshopping.

Drew is a proud alum of the Time Step Symposium dance arranging workshop and a member of the BMI Lehman Engel Musical Theatre Writing Advanced Songwriting Workshop.

**MUSIC TECHNOLOGY**

Much of Drew’s current work lies at the intersection of his experience in music direction, keyboard performance, orchestration, studio production, audio engineering, and app development in the role of Keyboard Programmer/Electronic Music Design. During the rehearsal/tech process for theatre productions, he programs Mainstage rigs, Kurzweils, electronic drum kits and pads, guitar modelers, complex click/track/timecode playback in Qlab, in fast-paced collaboration with composers, orchestrators, music supervisors, and sound designers.
